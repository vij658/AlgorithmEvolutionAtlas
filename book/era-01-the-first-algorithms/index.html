The First Algorithms

Nine steps, from a notched bone to a method for listing primes. Each one answered a pain the step before it left behind. Several still run, almost unchanged, inside the machines of 2026.

Field note from the visiting historian. Before writing, people cut notches in bone. By the time they built libraries, they had written down procedures that work for any number.
