The Algorithm Evolution Atlas · Era 2 · c. 200 BCE to 1424 CE

Algorithms Travel

Eleven steps, from columns of rods on a Chinese counting board to π computed to sixteen decimal places in Samarkand. The procedures cross languages and number systems, and several are found twice, centuries apart.

Field note from the visiting historian. In the first era, procedures were invented in a few places. In this one they travel: from China and India to Baghdad, and from Baghdad to Pisa. Along the way they gain a sign for nothing, a name, and the first infinite series.
