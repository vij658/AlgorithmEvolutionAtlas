# Era 2 — Algorithms Travel (plan)

*c. 200 BCE to c. 1500 CE. Draft topic list, in strict order of time. Dates are working dates to be checked in the reference catalog; the catalog's certainty labels win where they differ.*

> **Field note from the visiting historian.** In the first era, procedures were invented in a few places and written on clay, papyrus and parchment. In this one they travel: from China and India to Baghdad, and from Baghdad to Pisa. Along the way they gain a sign for nothing, a name ("algorithm" comes from al-Khwarizmi), and the first infinite series.

## Decisions after the reference catalog

- The *Nine Chapters* and the *Sunzi Suanjing* are dated as ranges, labelled **disputed**.
- The Bakhshali manuscript stays in topic 4's notes, not its date line: its radiocarbon dates are disputed.
- The pulverizer is not a separate topic. It is covered in Era 1, topic 7, and topic 5 builds on it.
- The sources credit the cyclic chakravala step to Jayadeva, quoted in a text of 1073, before Bhaskara II; topic 5 is named for the method, not for one author.
- Horner's method appears in Qin Jiushao's book of 1247, 572 years before Horner's paper of 1819: "nearly six centuries", not "600 years".
