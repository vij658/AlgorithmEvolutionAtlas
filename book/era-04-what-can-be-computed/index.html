The Algorithm Evolution Atlas · Era 4 · 1895 to 1950

What Can Be Computed

Thirteen steps, from Hilbert's questions of 1900 to Hamming's error-correcting codes of 1950. Along the way, computation gets a definition, programs become numbers, and machines are set to work on chance, planning, secrecy and their own mistakes.

Field note from the visiting historian. In the third era, procedures were put into tables and machines. In this one, mathematicians ask what a procedure is, prove that some questions have none, and build the first computers that keep their programs in memory.
