The Algorithm Evolution Atlas · Era 5 · 1949 to 1965

The Programmer's Craft

Thirteen steps, from the subroutine of 1949 to Edmonds's "good" algorithms of 1965. Along the way, programs are built from shared parts and translated by the machine itself, memory is organised into lists, tables, trees and heaps, a machine learns from examples, and efficiency gets a definition.

Field note from the visiting historian. In the fourth era, the species learned what a procedure is and built machines that keep procedures in memory. In this one, they learn how to write procedures well, and invent the tools of a new craft.
