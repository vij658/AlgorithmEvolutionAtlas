The Algorithm Evolution Atlas · Era 6 · 1965 to 1978

Hard Problems and Public Keys

Thirteen steps, from the fast Fourier transform of 1965 to Lamport's clocks of 1978. Along the way, programs share memory safely, are built from objects, search with an estimate, store data in tables and trees made for disks, find that a whole family of problems stands or falls together, exchange keys in public, search and compress text without a model, and agree on what "before" means.

Field note from the visiting historian. In the fifth era, the species learned to write programs well. In this one, programs run side by side, talk across networks and keep secrets, and the species learns that some problems may have no fast method at all.
