The Algorithm Evolution Atlas · Era 9 · 2009 to 2026

Learning at Scale

Thirteen steps, from fully homomorphic encryption in 2009 to algorithms found by machines in 2022 and 2025. Along the way, a computer adds and multiplies numbers it cannot see, a network learns to recognize photographs, words become vectors, a consensus algorithm is designed to be understood, an optimizer sets its own step sizes, text is cut into tokens, a shortcut lets a signal through a hundred layers, random games beat the best Go players, attention replaces recurrence, a key exchange is built to survive quantum computers, pictures are drawn out of noise, attention is computed without its table, and a search finds a matrix multiplication with 47 products where people had found 49.

Field note from the visiting historian. In the eighth era, the species built a Web, read its genome and agreed on a ledger. In this one an old idea, the network that learns from examples, suddenly works at scale, and much of the era's algorithmic work goes into making that learning possible; alongside it the species learns to compute on data it cannot read, prepares its cryptography for quantum computers, and sets its machines to search for algorithms.
